# Verification and limitations

These are records of individual prototype trials on 2026-11-03, not a reliability study. They are separate runs; their results must not be combined into one continuous demonstration.

- **Real Muse Spark, 100 virtual drones:** a simulated obstacle affected 20 drones. In one run both model roles proposed the original-facility route; after app-side checks, the screen showed 100 virtual drop-offs and 100 returns. In a separate run the final role selected no candidate, so 20 drones stopped while 80 completed simulated drop-off and return. Cargo was unspecified in both.
- **Real Muse Voice Transcribe and Spark, synthetic speech:** Japanese synthesized audio was supplied to the recording path. The transcription of a request for twelve boxes of water was confirmed in the UI; 12 virtual drones were assigned and 88 held in reserve. Both Spark roles selected no candidate, so 12 stopped and zero boxes were reported delivered. This is not a real microphone-to-completion test.
- **Time limit:** one building-model sampling operation did not finish within 15 seconds, and the affected virtual drones stopped. The underlying reason was not established. A prior sampling operation can continue after a timeout and block later checks on the sampling queue. A separate artificial timeout test confirmed a descriptive stop message; it was not a live model outage.
- **Automated checks:** on 2026-11-04 this candidate passed 813 automated tests after a clean `npm ci`. These include deterministic cases and mocks and do not prove live model availability, true flight safety or real device behavior.

The aerial imagery is not live. Flood layers represent an assumed event, not observed current flooding. The displayed obstacle is generated by the app. Point samples do not establish what lies between points, the presence of wires, weather suitability, flight authorization, aircraft separation, or facility acceptance. No actual drones, deliveries or rescue decisions were tested. Past simulation records are reference information and never establish present safety.
